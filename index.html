    How to play

    Get from the start word to the goal word in as few steps as possible.

    • Each step, type any noun and choose + to add it to your current word or − to subtract it.
    • Every word is a vector of numbers. The game adds or subtracts the two vectors, then lands on the noun closest to the result. That becomes your new word.
    • Typing -word or +word picks the operation for you.

    Example

    king − man → queen

    Closeness to the goal

    • Cold: not among the goal's 1,000 nearest words
    • Warm: in the goal's 1,000 nearest words
    • Hot: in the goal's 100 nearest words
    • Goal: you made it!
